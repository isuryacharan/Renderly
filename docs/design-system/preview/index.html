Renderly design system

Live preview of the tokens and component styles in packages/ui. Rules are in docs/design-system/README.md.

Colour

Neutral chrome, one amber accent, four clip colours, three status colours. Values change with the theme switch above.

viewerSurround of the video viewer and the timeline track area. Strictly neutral so it never tints the picture.
bgWindow background and the gaps between panels.
surfacePanels: library, inspector, timeline headers, toolbars.
surface-raisedSecondary buttons, menus, popovers and dialogs. Not for fields; those use field.
surface-hoverHover fill for rows, ghost buttons and menu items. Pressed state of raised controls.
fieldFill of text, number and select fields and of unchecked checkboxes: a recessed well on surface or surface-raised. Text on it is text; placeholders and units are text-tertiary.
grooveUnfilled part of slider tracks and progress bars, and the off state of a switch.
thumbSlider and switch knobs, in both themes. Always carries shadow-thumb so it separates from light surfaces.
borderHairline dividers between panels, sections and rows, and the resting edge of text, number and select fields (whose field fill and label also mark them).
border-strongHover edge of fields; resting edge of checkboxes. Holds 3:1 on surface, surface-raised and field in both themes.
textPrimary text and icons on bg, surface, surface-raised and surface-hover.
text-secondaryLabels, property names and inactive tabs on bg, surface and surface-raised.
text-tertiaryHints, units, placeholders and shortcut keys on bg, surface and surface-raised.
text-disabledDisabled labels and icons only. Below text contrast on purpose; never use for readable content.
accentThe one accent, amber: primary button fill, checked boxes and switches, slider fill, active tab underline, highlighted menu item, inspector keyframes. Bright amber in the dark theme, a deeper orange in the light theme so it still holds 3:1 as a mark on light surfaces. Pair with on-accent.
accent-hoverHover and pressed fill of accent controls. Pair with on-accent.
on-accentText and icons on accent and accent-hover fills: near-black on the bright amber of the dark theme, white on the deeper orange of the light theme. Never assume it is white.
accent-textAccent-coloured text and icons on bg, surface and surface-raised: links, active tool icon, animated-property keyframe outline.
accent-subtleTinted fill behind a selected row or an active toggle, and the halo around a focused field or a hovered slider knob. Text on it stays text or accent-text.
focusKeyboard focus ring, 2px solid, offset 1px. Holds 3:1 on every surface.
selectionSelection outline and keyframe diamonds drawn on timeline clips and on the viewer picture. The same bright amber in both themes, because clips and footage are dark-to-mid in either theme. Always edged with selection-edge.
selection-edge1px line inside a selection outline and around a keyframe diamond or overlay handle, so the amber separates from any clip colour, thumbnail or footage behind it.
playheadPlayhead line and handle on the timeline and on scrub bars. Highest contrast mark in the app; nothing else uses it.
dangerError text and icons on bg, surface and surface-raised; destructive menu items; clipping in meters. Always with a word or an icon.
warningWarning text and icons on bg, surface and surface-raised: offline media, dropped frames, missing packs. Yellow, to stay apart from the amber accent; because the two are neighbours it must always come with a warning icon and a word.
successSuccess text and icons on bg, surface and surface-raised: saved, export finished. Sits toward blue so it is not confused with danger. Always with a word or an icon.
clip-videoFill of video and image clips on the timeline. Label in on-clip.
clip-audioFill of audio clips and waveform backgrounds. Label in on-clip.
clip-textFill of text and caption clips. Label in on-clip.
clip-effectFill of effect and adjustment-layer clips. Label in on-clip.
on-clipClip names, icons, waveforms and thumbnails' overlays on any clip fill.
scrimDimmed layer behind dialogs.
